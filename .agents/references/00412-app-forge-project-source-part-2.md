## Verified foundation (slice 16: Graphics groups, transforms and the leave dialog, 2026-11-15)

Promoted from spec 00015 after Proofs P-43 to P-46 passed. The spec holds the decisions (O-01 to O-10), the Gaps (O-G1 to O-G4), the Fireworks research, the journeys and the evidence.

- Leaving an unsaved Graphics project goes through an in-app dialog, not `window.confirm`: the desktop webview (wry on WebKit) has no JavaScript dialogs and answers `confirm` with false in about a millisecond (the `FORGE_CONFIRM_TEST` hook), so the source's `confirmDiscard` and `confirmLeave` always answered "keep" and New, Open and the home prompt did nothing while a document was open. `guard_leave(editor, action, proceed)` runs `proceed` at once when nothing is unsaved, else holds a `LeaveRequest { action, proceed }` that the root-level `LeaveDialog` resolves with Save… (proceeds only after a real save, through `save_project_then`), Don't Save or Cancel; shortcuts and paste pause while it is open. The home lists the open project first under Recent Documents ("Continue <name>", a composite thumbnail, size, layers, "unsaved"/"open"; its × closes the project through the guard); the old Continue button is gone. The desktop command delivery was proven sound by `FORGE_MENU_TEST` (`menu: new → graphics`, `bridge: delivered shell/menu to graphics`); a command with no app on screen is dropped by design and those items are disabled.
- Groups are flat and Fireworks-like: `LayerBase.group: Option<String>` names a `Document.groups` entry (`Group { id, name, mask }`), members are contiguous, and `Document::normalize_groups` restores the run at the topmost member and drops empty groups after every `set_doc`. Old `.opng` files have neither `group` nor `groups` and files with groups still open flattened in the TypeScript app. ⌘G groups, ⇧⌘G ungroups, ⌥⌘G groups as a mask; a click on a member selects the whole group (`expand_groups`), Alt-click one layer, the marquee takes touched groups whole; a new layer never lands inside a run and duplicating a whole group makes a new group. The Layers panel shows a group row (fold, group-wide eye and lock, rename, drag as one via `reorder_group`) above indented members; dragging a member out of the run leaves the group, dropped between two members joins it. A mask group draws as one in `render_document` (`draw_masked_group`): the lower members into an offscreen document-sized canvas, the top member with `destination-in` (its opacity applies, its blend and effects do not), composited once; the mask layer is tagged "mask".
- A layer carries rotation and skew as one map: `LayerBase.skew_x`, `skew_y` (degrees, `skewX`/`skewY`, absent when 0) shear by their tangents about the bounds centre inside the rotation (`geometry::layer_transform` → `Transform`). Rendering (`ctx.transform`), hit testing (`transform().inverse()`), the handles (`handle_screen_points`), resizing (`ResizeSession` through the map) and the axis-aligned box (`transformed_aabb`) all go through it. The Skew tool (Q): a side handle shears along its edge with the opposite edge held (`skew_by_handle`), a corner shears both ways, Shift snaps to 15°, ±89°. The Distort tool (D) on rectangles, ellipses and paths only: `distortable` bakes any rotation and skew into a path of the shape's outline (`shape_outline`: corners, rounded corners as 8-segment arcs, a 64-gon ellipse) and a corner or side drag pulls every point by `bilinear_map`. The transform tools grab the selection's handles on the same terms the pointer shows them (any unlocked layer, visible or not).
- The multi-selection panel (`MultiProps`) edits the fields the selected layers share as one layer: the union box (X, Y move all; W, H scale all about the union origin, aspect link), rotation, skew X/Y, opacity, fill (shapes and texts), stroke and width (shapes), corner radius (rectangles), Closed (paths), font, size, weight, italic, leading, align (texts), Reset Sizes (bitmaps), Merge to Bitmap and effects; a field reads "Mixed" where the layers differ and the first change sets them all (`NumberField`'s `mixed`). A selection that is exactly one group shows the group's name, its mask switch and Ungroup; any other multi-selection offers Group and Group as Mask.
- Pasting an image onto the canvas works again: a window `paste` listener (installed once with the keyboard) sends image files on the clipboard through the drop intake (`receive_files` → `graphics/open-bytes` → `import_replies`, centred as layers) and otherwise pastes the in-app clipboard; ⌘V no longer calls `preventDefault` on keydown, which had stopped the browser from firing `paste`. The agent sees `group`, `groupName`, `mask`, `skewX`, `skewY` in `get_canvas` and `update_layers` accepts the skews (no grouping tool yet, Gap O-G4).

## Verified foundation (slice 17: Graphics vector craft, 2026-11-15)

Promoted from spec 00016 after Proofs P-47 to P-49 passed. The spec holds the decisions (P-01 to P-11), the Gaps (P-G1 to P-G6), the reading of the thirteen reference styles, the journeys and the evidence.

- Files dropped anywhere on the Graphics frame take the paste route: the window's `dragover`/`drop` listeners call `receive_files` (in the editor files become layers, on the home a single image opens as a document, Alt opens one as a document); the desktop window is built with `disable_drag_drop_handler()` because Tauri's own handler otherwise swallows file drops on macOS, so no app sees a DOM `drop` without it.
- The right-click menu (`components/context_menu.rs`, `editor.context_menu`): Cut, Copy, Paste (when the in-app clipboard holds something), Duplicate, Delete; Group, Group as Mask, Ungroup, Select Group; Bring to Front/Forward, Send Backward/to Back; Lock or Unlock, Hide or Show; Merge or Convert to Bitmap; Select All, Deselect. A right-click on a layer outside the selection selects it first (its whole group on the canvas); the menu clamps to the window and closes on a click elsewhere, Escape, blur or a pick; the keyboard map stands down while it is open (else Escape would deselect). The Properties panel keeps a group's name and mask switch and points at the menu for the actions.
- Alignment: `align_to_canvas` (on by default) moves several layers as one block so their union meets the canvas edge or centre; off, they align to each other. The scope toggle sits at the end of the align row (Fireworks' "To Canvas").
- Painting: `PaintSession` paints into a scratch canvas and `Recompose` lays it over the base at the stroke's opacity (through the pixel selection's mask when there is one), so overlapping dabs never exceed the opacity and the eraser cuts cleanly. An upright bitmap paints on a document-sized working canvas holding its pixels at their place and display size, and the layer is trimmed to its opaque bounds when the stroke ends, so the box follows the paint and strokes may run past the old box; a rotated or skewed bitmap paints in its own pixel space.
- The brush (`brush.rs`): `Brush { hardness, flow, opacity, spacing, tip: Round | Flat { angle, ratio }, grain: None | Noise | Speckle | Streak, texture, jitter, wet_edge, follow }` beside `brush_size`; `Dabs` builds sprite variants (the colour under a radial alpha edge, a wet rim, a grain mask) and stamps them along each segment at the spacing with carry-over; `presets()` gives Fireworks' categories (Air Brush, Basic, Calligraphy, Charcoal, Crayon, Felt Tip, Oil, Pencil, Watercolor) with two or three variants each; `apply_brush_preset` sets the settings and the tip size; any slider makes "Custom". The eraser is a round tip at the brush's softness and opacity. The panel (`BrushProps`) shows the preset by category, tip size, edge softness, flow, opacity, texture kind and amount, flat-nib angle and thickness, wet edge and scatter.
- Curves: `PathLayer.d` holds SVG path data relative to the layer; `graphics_model::svg_path` parses M L H V C S Q T A Z (absolute, relative, implicit repeats; arcs as cubics), flattens, bounds, maps and prints; `points` is kept as the flattened outline (`sync_points`, `set_commands`) so bounds, hit testing, old readers and the TypeScript app keep working. The renderer draws the commands; `set_layer_bounds`, the transform and Distort map control points so curves stay curves; the pen still draws polylines.
- Paints: a fill or stroke string may be a CSS gradient (`graphics_model::paint`: linear with an angle or direction, radial from the centre to the farthest corner; stop offsets placed as CSS does); `render::apply_paint` builds the canvas gradient over the layer's own box (text too). `ShapeStyle::{stroke_cap, stroke_join, stroke_dash}` apply at draw time (`strokeCap`, `strokeJoin`, `strokeDash`). The colour field (`ColorInput allow_gradient`) shows a gradient in its swatch, holds the CSS in its text box and opens an inline editor (kind, angle, stops); `StrokeOptions` sets cap, join and dash.
- The agent: `add_layer` and `update_layers` take `d` (document coordinates, rebased), `strokeCap`, `strokeJoin`, `strokeDash`, `skewX`, `skewY` and gradient strings; `get_canvas` returns `d` in document coordinates and the options; `group_layers { ids, name, mask }` and `ungroup_layers { ids }` are tools; `VECTOR_PROMPT` follows the base prompt with the capabilities and a method for vector illustration. The preview answers `harness:command { command }` with `harness:result`, so proofs can draw without a provider.

## Verified foundation (slice 19: Graphics inking and effects, 2026-11-15)

Promoted from spec 00018 after Proofs P-53 to P-55 passed. The spec holds the decisions (R-01 to R-08), the Gaps (R-G1 to R-G3), the research, the journeys and the evidence.

- The Graphics backend's file dialogs (`files::choose`) run `osascript` with `tell application "System Events" to activate` first and, when macOS refuses that (-1743, "not authorized": the Automation permission), run the dialog again without it; both refused, the error names the setting. `write_atomic` explains a permission refusal (the Files and Folders setting) and a missing folder.
- Inked lines: `ShapeStyle::stroke_widths` (`strokeWidths`, paths only) are widths at evenly spaced stations along the path; `geometry::variable_stroke_outline` offsets the centreline (the flattened points) by half the interpolated width along each vertex normal and the renderer fills the polygon with the stroke paint instead of stroking; the hit test takes the widest point; `Editor::set_stroke_widths` and the panel's "Stroke widths (inked)" field set them.
- `Effect::Roughen { size, amount }`: `build_shape_path` draws `geometry::roughen` of the shape's outline (resampled every `size` px, each point pushed along its normal by up to `amount`, seeded by the point count) so rendering and hit testing agree; shapes and paths only. `Effect::Blur { radius }`: `draw_layer` sets the canvas filter `blur(radius px)` outside raw passes; `effect_padding` adds three radii and the roughen amount. Both are in the Add menu (Roughen for shapes) and the effect editor (Size and Amount; Radius).
- `duplicate_layers { ids, count, dx, dy, rotate, scale, jitter, name }`: copies moved, turned about the source's centre and scaled to their step with a seeded jitter, inserted above the source, named `name N`, bitmaps' pixels copied, one undo step, the copies selected. `effects` on `add_layer` and `update_layers` take `{ type, ... }` objects (`commands::effects_of`: the app's defaults under the given fields; `null` clears); `get_canvas` returns the effect objects whole and `strokeWidths`.
- `VECTOR_PROMPT` names inked lines, the effects (drop-shadow, glow, blur, roughen, blend, hsb) and duplicates; Kazu's method draws strands as inked paths duplicated into fans, roughens fur and blurs the halo; Rex tapers interior lines and roughens the mane.

- The toolbox (`toolbox::GROUPS`) is four captioned groups two tools to a row: Select (Pointer, Box, Ellipse, Lasso, Polygon Lasso, Magic wand, Skew, Distort), Vector (Polygon, Star, Line, Pen, Text), Paint (Pencil, Brush, Eraser, Bucket, Eyedropper), View (Zoom, Hand); the swatches follow on the same two-column grid (the swatch in one cell, the "no colour" button in the other). The Lasso (`ToolId::Lasso`, key O) collects the drag's path (`editor.lasso` draws it dashed, closed back to its start) and `flood::polygon_selection` rasterises it even-odd into a `PixelSelection` combined with the existing one through `tools::apply_lasso` (Shift adds, Alt subtracts). The Polygon Lasso (`ToolId::PolyLasso`, O again: the two share the key) is clicked like the pen: `tools::POLY_LASSO` keeps the corners, the starting selection and the combine mode of the first click, `editor.lasso` mirrors the corners plus the rubber band; a click on the first corner, a double-click or Enter closes it (`finish_poly_lasso`), Esc (`cancel_poly_lasso`) or another tool (`set_tool` clears `editor.lasso`) abandons it.
- `state::tool_applies(tool, &selected)` decides which tools can act: the Eraser needs a bitmap selected; Box, Ellipse, Lasso and Magic wand a bitmap or nothing; Distort a vector or nothing; the rest always. `Editor::tool_enabled[_tracked]` wraps it: the toolbox greys the button (the title says why), the key map and `pointer_down` notify instead of acting, and the active tool falls back to the Pointer when the selection makes it unusable.
- `Editor::flip_selected(horizontal)` mirrors the selection as one block across the union box's centre (centres mirrored, rotation and skews negated, path points or `d` mirrored in the layer's box, bitmap pixels redrawn flipped, rectangles and ellipses kept, text layers left with a note). Properties' Transform section has Flip horizontal and Flip vertical; the agent's `flip_layers { ids, axis }` is the same method.
- Properties is `PropsSection`s whose toggles live in `editor.props_toggled` for the session (open = default XOR toggled): one layer shows Layer, Position & size, Transform, Fill & stroke, Stroke details (closed by default), Text or Bitmap, Effects; several layers show the group fields, then the same sections with Merge to Bitmap under Layer and Reset Sizes under Bitmap. `tool_panel_title(editor)` names the Tool panel `SidePanels` mounts above Properties (Shape for Polygon, Star, Line and Pen until a single shape is selected; Brush, Eraser, Magic wand, Paint bucket, Text; none for tools without settings: the Pointer, the marquees, the lassos, the pencil, Hand, Zoom, Eyedropper, Skew and Distort); `ToolProps` is its body. The slot (`.tool-slot`) is `flex: 0 0 auto; max-height: 45%` with the panel filling it and the body scrolling past the cap, and the Properties slot has a zero flex basis so its content never squeezes the Tool panel.
- `get_canvas` carries `pixelSelection { x, y, width, height, pixels }` when the person has one.

## Verified foundation (slice 22: Graphics point editing, bucket softness and chrome, 2026-11-16)

Promoted from spec 00021 after Proofs P-63 and P-64 passed. The spec holds the decisions (U-01 to U-08), the Gaps (U-G1 to U-G3), the research, the journeys and the evidence.

- Point editing (`editor.editing_points: Option<PointEdit { id, selected }>`): a double-click with the Pointer on a path, a rectangle or an ellipse calls `Editor::begin_point_edit` (a curve-free path as it is; a radius-0 rectangle becomes a four-point path; curves, rounded rectangles, ellipses, text and bitmaps decline with a note). The canvas draws the outline along the sides and a square handle per point (the picked one filled) instead of the box; `tools::point_handles` maps the points to the screen. A press on a handle starts `PointDragSession` (the pointer through the inverse of the start transform into the path's space), a double-click on a side inserts the projected point (`side_hit`), Delete removes the picked point (three kept on a closed path, two on an open one), the arrows nudge it; Esc, a selection change (an effect in `EditorScreen`) or `set_tool` end it. `tools::refit_path` re-origins the points to their minimum and, for a rotated or skewed path, shifts the base by `(M − I)(c′ − c)` so unmoved points stay put.
- The Paint Bucket's `editor.bucket_softness` (0 to 50 px) and `fill_area { softness? }`: `flood::soften_mask` (a box blur run twice over the samples that exist, the flood's 0/1 mask read as coverage) and `bucket_fill`'s `blend_over` blend the colour by coverage; 0 is the hard fill.
- `EditorScreen` is a row of the main column (top bar, workspace, status bar) and `SidePanels`, so the side panels run the window's full height; the top bar holds Home and the document name only; `.tool-foot` at the bottom of the toolbox holds Keyboard Shortcuts (`keyboard` icon) and About (`help`). `.row` bottom-aligns and a button beside a field is 26 px like the field's input.
- `paint_stroke { layer }` resolves names through `resolve_one` (the single-entry form of `resolve_ids`); an unknown name fails with a note.
