# OfficePress — user-provided brand and product descriptions

Provenance: user source message in this OfficePress KB ingestion conversation; accepted for ingestion on 2026-10-01. Formatting and HTML entities are normalized; wording of the descriptions is retained. These are product descriptions, not independently verified deployment claims.

## Project and brand

Project: OfficePress

Catch Phrases:

- Make a well-run business easier to build.
- Publishing the building blocks of a well-run business.
- A capable back office within reach of every business.

Product Promise:

OfficePress provides ready-made tools for the work behind a business—designed to work together, adopted as needed, and run under the company’s control.

Brand Meaning:

**Office:** the shortened form of **back office**—the operational functions that support a business.

**Press:** Published apps that different companies can adopt and put to work in their own setups.

## Inbox

Email inbox and workflow application for small teams. It connects to existing mail accounts through standard POP3 and SMTP, securely downloads complete messages into a tenant-isolated local store, and presents them through three complementary views: Inbox for thread-based email triage, Chat for participant-based conversations and contact requests, Board for managing emails as workflow cards with columns, checklists, SLA timers, and automations

## Calendar

Scheduling hub for managing your time, inviting people to meetings, and creating events that others can register to attend. You can also share your availability so people can request a meeting with you.

## Drive

A familiar file explorer for personal and app-managed files. Supported files open in the relevant Productivity app; other OfficePress apps can register their own files in Drive.

## Meet

A dedicated space for live video meetings and calls, connected to Calendar invitations.

## Agent

A personal workspace for setting up and working with AI agents.

## Resourcing

An HR operations workspace for managing organization structure, role profiles, personnel, and work assignments in one place. HR teams can coordinate stage-based workflows, collect information through forms, prepare messages and documents, and manage job posts and applicants through Careers.

## Procurement

A vendor and purchasing workspace for accreditation, offers, requisitions, purchase orders, RFQs, and quotes from approved vendors.

## Accounting

A tool for tracking money owed alongside actual cash flow. Its features covers bank accounts, receivables and disbursements, and debit or credit transactions. Transactions can be linked to an obligation to track partial payments and final settlement, while account activity is intended to read like a bank statement.

## Support

A shared workspace for internal support teams to manage customer conversations from email, Facebook Messenger, and WhatsApp. Teams can organize tickets by department and inbox, assign ownership, reply or add internal notes, and track each conversation through resolution and closure. Inbox, Chat, and Board views support different ways of working, while saved replies, checklists, and automation help with repeatable tasks.

## Approvals

A tool for getting business requests—such as purchases, travel, or equipment spend—reviewed by the right people. A requester drafts a proposal, assigns approvers and reviewers, and submits it. Approvers decide with the request’s details in front of them; reviewers can comment. The product keeps a durable record of each review round, decision, and comment so people can see what happened later.

## Chat

An internal communication tool that allows custom plugins, bots and app integrations. Supports media sharing, channels, group discussions, and agent collaboration

## Clients

A workflow tracker for client profiles, leads, assignments, and relationship workflows shown through records and a workflow board.

## Content

A headless content management system for organizing and publishing pages, categories, and associated files across multiple websites.

## Sign

A digital document-signing tool built around cryptographic provability.

## Tables

SQL-native spreadsheet for internal teams. It lets people browse databases and schemas as folders, open tables and views as files, and work with records in a familiar grid. The current application includes typed editing, saved views, imports and CSV exports, live updates, activity monitoring, and governed AI access through MCP. PostgreSQL remains the source of truth for data, permissions, and constraints.

## Forms

A builder for dynamic forms and templates, with submission, response, and generated-file workflows.

## Whiteboards

A freeform drawing canvas for quick visual thinking.

## Diagrams

An editor for structured diagrams such as processes, systems, and relationships.

## Products

A workspace for managing a shared commerce catalogue across suppliers and stores. Administrators can view products, offers, and profiles across the catalogue, then open a profile’s workspace to create product content, organize categories and attributes, build variants, and curate collections. Sellers can turn published products or quotes available to them into priced offers, assemble bundles, and control which profiles are eligible to take up an offer.

## Orders

A tracker for orders from intake through fulfillment. Staff can create orders manually, while origin systems can submit them through an authenticated API. Each order contains one or more shipments, and each shipment follows its own configurable workflow cards with columns, assigned owner, tasks, SLA, and activity history. Stage automations can send messages, update work, and generate tracking through configured logistics providers. OPS also manages customer records, staff, reusable messages, and provider connections.

## Inventory

An tracker for managing inventory records for clients and stock activity.

## Payments

Create order forms, invoice and payment links. Clients can pay using your configured payment options.

## Fulfillments

A 3PL accreditation manager for waybill printing, pickups and deliveries from approved vendors.
