# OfficePress

OfficePress publishes the building blocks of a well-run business: ready-made tools for the operational work behind a company.

## Product promise

> OfficePress provides ready-made tools for the work behind a business—designed to work together, adopted as needed, and run under the company’s control.

“Office” is the shortened form of “back office”: the operational functions that support a business. “Press” means published apps that different companies can adopt and put to work in their own setups.

The suite is designed for incremental adoption. Keep the company-control promise and interoperability in product explanations. Do not infer a particular license, price, hosting architecture, security certification or production capability from that promise.

## Positioning language

- Make a well-run business easier to build.
- Publishing the building blocks of a well-run business.
- A capable back office within reach of every business.
